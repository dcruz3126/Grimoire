## Ingredients

- 3lbs Pernil
- 3 tbsp achiote
- 2 tbsp Adobo
- 2 tbsp Sazon
- 1/2 cup sofrito
- 1/2 can Tomato Sauce 
- 4 cups chicken stock

## Instructions

1. Cut pernil into small bite size chunks
2. Add achiote, adobo, sazon, mix well.
3. Add sofrito, mix well. Cook for 6-8 min
4. Add tomato sauce and chicken stock, taste for salt. 
5. Cook in instapot for 3 min
6. Add potatoes, cook 3 more min.
